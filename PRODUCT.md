# Product

## Platform
web

## Users
Fans of the A1esports organization.

## Product Purpose
An esports organization website designed to engage fans, showcase players, merchandise, achievements, and social stats.

## Positioning
A high-quality, professional esports brand presence that breaks away from generic, "AI-generated" templates.

## Operating Context
Fans interacting with the team's digital presence via web browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must preserve existing functions and components (Achievements, SocialGrid, PlayerSection, Merchandise, AboutUs).
- The hero section must be entirely removed.
- Technical stack: Next.js (React), Tailwind CSS, framer-motion.

## Brand Commitments
The brand color is **Purple**.

## Evidence on Hand
